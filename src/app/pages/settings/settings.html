Privacy

Your data is encrypted in the browser - with a key derived from your account password - before it's ever sent to Supabase. Supabase only ever stores ciphertext; nothing leaves your device unencrypted.

Danger Zone

Permanently erases everything in your account - banks, wallets, cards, transactions, fixed deposits, and categories - and starts it over empty. Your username and password are unaffected. This cannot be undone; there is no recovery key.
