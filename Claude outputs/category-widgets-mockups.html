Category widgets — three alternatives to Category Pace

Illustrative numbers built on the same account: this month's expenses split across Bills & Utilities, Food & Drink, Groceries, Transport and Entertainment. Each answers a different question than "is this normal" — pick whichever's most useful day to day, or mix pieces.

Answers "where did my money go" rather than "is this normal" — the two are genuinely different questions and this app doesn't currently have a lightweight glance version of the first one (Reports has the full top-6 version, but that's a page away). Each slice would use the category's own saved color, same as everywhere else in the app, so no new color palette is needed.

Option B

Category Extremes vs your 3-mo avg

Furthest over Bills & Utilities 14× RM722.40 vs RM52.00 avg
Furthest under Transport -35% RM117.00 vs RM180.00 avg

Same underlying math as Category Pace, just distilled to the two extremes instead of a scanning list of four. Reads in under a second: one thing ran hot, one thing ran cold. Trades away visibility into the "on pace" middle categories, which the full Category Pace list still shows if you want that detail.
